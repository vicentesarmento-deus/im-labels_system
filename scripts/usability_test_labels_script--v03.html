Facilitation rules

Time box and help

  • DoEach task has 3 minutes. Do not help before then.
  • DoIf the participant asks for help, say: What would you do if I weren’t here?
  • DoAt 3:00, or if they say they’d give up, say: Thanks, that’s very helpful. Let’s go on to the next one. Mark the task as not completed.

How to facilitate

  • DoYour primary tools are open-ended questions and silence. Your role is to keep them talking about what they see and think in detail.
  • DoDo not interrupt or fill a pause quickly; they are often formulating a key thought.
  • DoDon’t say too much, but if there’s a long silence, you can ask ‘who’, ‘what’, ‘when’, ‘which’, ‘how.’
  • DoUsually you don’t even have to finish your sentence to get them talking again.

During a task, use these neutral phrases:

  • What are you looking at?
  • What are you thinking?
  • You paused. What’s on your mind?
  • What else did you notice?
  • Can you say a bit more about that?
  • DoDon’t confirm what they say by nodding or encouraging: ‘yes’, ‘indeed’. (This is hard for people that are good at active listening ;)
